# v5.5 Focus Anchor Refinement

- Removed bold weight from the green recognition-point character.
- Added two short green fixation markers above and below the recognition point.
- Markers leave a gap around the word and do not cross through the text.
- Applies to both Flash center-focus rendering and the draggable Focus Anchor overlay.
- Existing independent Focus Anchor font-size and drag-position behavior are unchanged.
