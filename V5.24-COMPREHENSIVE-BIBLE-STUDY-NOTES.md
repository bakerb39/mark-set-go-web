# v5.24 Comprehensive Bible Study

- Adds Bible Study tabs for Text, Commentary, Cross References, Profiles, and Notes.
- Dynamically loads available English public-domain/open commentaries.
- Dynamically loads open Bible datasets and defaults to a cross-reference dataset when available.
- Supports commentary chapter retrieval.
- Supports commentary profile discovery where available.
- Adds local study notes per translation/book/chapter.
- Adds Read Entire Book; chapters are combined into a reader document with a chapter TOC.
- Keeps Read Chapter and side-by-side translation comparison.
- Keeps AI Study / Great Ideas.
- Does not load an entire Bible into memory by default; full Bible browsing remains chapter/book based for performance.
