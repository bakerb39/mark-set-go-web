# v7.7.19 — My Library Bible Reload Fallback

- Adds a targeted fallback when a saved Bible chapter has progress metadata but its browser-only document payload is missing.
- Rebuilds the exact saved translation, book, and chapter through the existing Bible API.
- Reuses the saved title and source metadata, stores the rebuilt document locally, and continues through the existing resume path.
- Does not change the reader engine or core reader architecture.
