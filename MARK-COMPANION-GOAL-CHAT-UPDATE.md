# Mark Companion Goal Chat Update

This update moves reading-goal encouragement into the regular Ask Mark conversation.

## Behavior

- Mark adds a normal chat message with his avatar when a goal book is opened.
- A new update appears at most once per day, or sooner when progress increases by at least 5% or crosses 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%.
- Messages include progress, days remaining when a deadline exists, and a friendly reading observation or encouragement.
- Messages are retained locally and restored in Ask Mark for the active book.
