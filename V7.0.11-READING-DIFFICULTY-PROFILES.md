# v7.0.11 Reading Difficulty Profiles

Built from v7.0.10 stable-rendering rollback.

- Adds difficulty badges to public-library search results, My Library, Continue Reading, and My Reading.
- Full locally stored texts are sampled across the entire book and analyzed for vocabulary, syntax, conceptual density, proper-name density, technical signals, and narrative complexity.
- Search results without full text receive clearly labeled Estimated or Predicted profiles from catalog metadata, era, author, subjects, and description.
- Profiles include overall level, 0–100 score, eight dimensions, key reasons, confidence, and reading recommendations.
- Full-text ratings are cached locally and replace metadata estimates after a readable edition is opened or imported.
- Levels: Accessible, Moderate, Challenging, Advanced, Expert.
- Does not alter Reader rendering or v7.0.10 stability behavior.
