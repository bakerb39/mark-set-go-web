SONG OF SOLOMON
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Bible Guide
Biblical book: Song of Solomon

Love, desire, beauty, marriage, longing, mutuality, sexuality, poetry, absence, delight, and covenantal devotion.

HOW TO USE THIS BIBLE GUIDE
Use this guide alongside the biblical text. Read the assigned passages first whenever possible. The guide is designed to help with structure, literary context, theology, difficult passages, connections across Scripture, discussion, and comprehension—not to replace Scripture itself.

READING PRINCIPLES
Pay attention to genre. Poetry, wisdom sayings, lament, dialogue, and reflective argument communicate differently from narrative or legal prose. Distinguish description from endorsement, read passages in context, and allow the whole biblical canon to inform difficult texts.

PURPOSE AND PLACE IN SCRIPTURE

Song of Solomon is a collection or sequence of lyrical love poetry celebrating desire, beauty, longing, mutual delight, and the power of love. Jewish and Christian traditions have also read it allegorically, but its poetic celebration of human love deserves attention in its own right.

STUDY QUESTION
Why should the poem’s human love imagery be understood before moving to symbolic interpretations?

ALLEGORICAL READINGS

Jewish interpreters have read the Song in relation to God and Israel; Christian interpreters often read it in relation to Christ and the church or the soul.

CHRIST AND THE REST OF SCRIPTURE

The Song fits the Bible’s broader affirmation of marriage, covenant love, creation, desire, and faithful delight. Christian canonical reading can draw analogies to divine love while respecting literary genre.

STUDY QUESTION
