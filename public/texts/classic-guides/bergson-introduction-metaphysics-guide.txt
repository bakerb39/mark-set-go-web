An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide

AT A GLANCE

Bergson contrasts two ways of knowing. Analysis approaches a thing from outside by translating it into concepts and relations; intuition attempts to enter sympathetically into the thing and grasp its unique movement from within. Metaphysics, for Bergson, seeks the latter kind of knowledge.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Bergson think analysis can be accurate and useful while still failing to reach the thing itself?

ANALYSIS

Analysis breaks reality into elements, compares those elements, and represents them through symbols. This makes science, communication, and practical action possible, but it also substitutes representations for lived movement.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What is gained—and what is lost—when reality is translated into stable concepts?

INTUITION

Intuition is not a vague feeling or rejection of reason. Bergson presents it as an intellectual sympathy by which one enters into the unique duration of an object or experience rather than viewing it only from external standpoints.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How is Bergsonian intuition different from impulse or private opinion?

DURATION

Duration is lived time: qualitative, continuous, and internally flowing. It is not identical with clock time, which spatializes time into measurable units.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Bergson think measured time can conceal the character of experienced time?

CHANGE

Ordinary concepts often treat change as a sequence of stable states. Bergson argues instead that real becoming is continuous and cannot be fully reconstructed from snapshots.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Can motion be explained by a collection of immobile states?

THE SELF

The inner self is not simply a bundle of fixed psychological states. Memory, expectation, emotion, and choice interpenetrate within duration.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How does a durational self differ from a self described as a list of properties?

CONCEPTS

Concepts are necessary instruments, but they are general and repeatable whereas concrete reality is singular and mobile. Metaphysical error occurs when tools of abstraction are mistaken for the whole of reality.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
When does a useful abstraction become a distortion?

SPACE AND TIME

Bergson argues that intellect tends to model time on space: divisible, homogeneous, and externally ordered. Duration resists this spatial treatment.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is the distinction between spatial order and temporal flow central to Bergson’s project?

SCIENCE AND METAPHYSICS

Bergson does not dismiss science. Science legitimately abstracts for prediction and action; metaphysics asks a different question about reality in its concrete becoming.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Can scientific and metaphysical knowledge be complementary rather than competitors?

FREEDOM

Bergson’s account of duration supports a distinctive account of freedom. A free act expresses the whole developing person rather than appearing as an isolated event mechanically caused by prior states.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What would freedom mean if the self itself is continuously becoming?

STYLE AND METHOD

Bergson relies heavily on images, analogies, and conceptual contrasts because he believes rigid definitions can freeze what philosophy is trying to understand.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why might philosophical style matter when the subject is movement and duration?

LEGACY

Bergson influenced phenomenology, process philosophy, literature, psychology, theology, and later debates about time and consciousness. Critics argue that his contrast between intuition and analysis can become too sharp.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Which part of Bergson’s challenge to conceptual thinking remains strongest today?
