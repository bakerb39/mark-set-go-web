An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide

AT A GLANCE

Barth’s essays insist that Christian theology begins not with human religious experience but with God’s self-revelation. Human words—including sermons, theology, and even our reading of Scripture—stand under judgment and receive authority only insofar as God freely uses them to bear witness to the divine Word.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What changes when revelation begins with God’s initiative rather than humanity’s religious search?

THE WORD OF GOD

For Barth, God is not an object available for ordinary human mastery. God speaks and makes himself known freely. Revelation therefore remains an event of grace rather than a possession controlled by theologians.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Barth resist treating revelation as information humans can simply own?

THE WORD OF MAN

Human speech about God is necessary and yet radically limited. Theology and preaching are responsible precisely because they can never identify themselves automatically with God’s own speech.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How can theology speak confidently while acknowledging its own fallibility?

REVELATION

Revelation exposes the distance between God and humanity while also overcoming that distance by divine initiative. This produces Barth’s characteristic tension between judgment and grace.

SCRIPTURE

Scripture bears authoritative witness to God’s revelation, but Barth’s emphasis prevents the interpreter from reducing biblical authority to a merely human possession or technique.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What is the difference between receiving Scripture as witness and treating it as an object under complete human control?

PREACHING

The preacher speaks ordinary human words while praying and trusting that God may use them as witness. This gives preaching both seriousness and humility.

RELIGION

Barth sharply criticizes religion when it becomes humanity’s attempt to reach or domesticate God. Even Christianity can become religious self-assertion if it forgets grace.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How can religious practice become a substitute for revelation?

FAITH

Faith is response rather than achievement. It is created and sustained by the God who addresses the hearer.

THEOLOGY

Theology is a disciplined act of listening, correction, and witness. It should remain answerable to revelation rather than to cultural prestige or philosophical fashion.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

CRISIS AND CULTURE

Barth’s early theology emerged partly from disillusionment with liberal Protestant confidence in culture, progress, and national moral authority after World War I.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

DIVINE FREEDOM

God remains free in revelation. Human systems cannot predict, possess, or compel the divine Word.

LEGACY

These essays helped launch twentieth-century dialectical and neo-orthodox theology. They remain influential for their insistence that Christianity must continually distinguish God’s revelation from human ideology.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Where is Barth’s warning about confusing God’s Word with human programs most relevant today?
