AN INTRODUCTION TO MATHEMATICS
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Alfred North Whitehead

A guide to mathematical abstraction, number, algebra, geometry, variables, functions, symbolism, and Whitehead’s vision of mathematics as a mode of thought.

This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

AT A GLANCE

Whitehead presents mathematics as a language of abstraction that reveals patterns across very different kinds of problems. The book aims less to teach advanced calculation than to show how mathematical ideas grow from number, measurement, geometry, symbolism, and generalization.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Whitehead treat mathematics as a way of organizing thought rather than merely a collection of techniques?

NUMBER AND COUNTING

Arithmetic begins with abstraction from particular objects toward number itself. Three apples and three stones differ physically but share a numerical structure.

LEGACY

The book remains valuable as an accessible philosophical introduction to mathematical thought. Its central theme is that mathematics trains the mind to recognize structure across changing contexts.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
