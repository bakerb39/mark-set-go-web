THE WANING OF THE MIDDLE AGES
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Johan Huizinga

A guide to late medieval culture, symbolism, ritual, courtly life, religion, violence, art, and the transition toward the Renaissance.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

HOW TO USE IT
Read one section at a time. Use Discuss with reading companion after each section for interpretation and Quiz me for a short comprehension check. Save the full-guide quiz for the end.

AT A GLANCE

Huizinga portrays the later Middle Ages not simply as an exhausted prelude to the Renaissance but as a culture of extraordinary emotional intensity, ritual formality, symbolism, piety, spectacle, and contradiction.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Huizinga resist a simple story of medieval decline giving way to modern progress?

A WORLD OF VIOLENT CONTRASTS

Late medieval life appears full of sharp oppositions: public cruelty and intense piety, ceremony and disorder, ascetic ideals and sensual display.
