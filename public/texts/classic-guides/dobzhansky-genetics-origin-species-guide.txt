GENETICS AND THE ORIGIN OF SPECIES
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Theodosius Dobzhansky

A guide to population genetics, variation, mutation, natural selection, speciation, reproductive isolation, and the modern evolutionary synthesis.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

AT A GLANCE

Dobzhansky helped unite Mendelian genetics with Darwinian evolution. Instead of treating species as fixed types or evolution as vague transformation, he explained evolutionary change through shifting gene frequencies in populations.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why was population genetics the bridge needed to unite Darwin and Mendel?
