THE NATURE OF LIFE
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by C. H. Waddington

A guide to development, heredity, organism, environment, evolution, and Waddington’s systems-oriented view of biological life.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

HOW TO USE IT
Read one section at a time. Use Discuss with reading companion after each section for interpretation and Quiz me for a short comprehension check. Save the full-guide quiz for the end.

AT A GLANCE

Waddington explores life as an organized developmental process shaped by genes, environment, and the structure of the organism. His work helped move biology beyond simple gene-by-gene thinking toward developmental systems and regulatory interactions.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is development more than the execution of a fixed genetic blueprint?
