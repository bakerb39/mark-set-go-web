WHAT IS METAPHYSICS?
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Martin Heidegger

A guide to Being, beings, the nothing, anxiety, science, transcendence, questioning, and Heidegger’s provocative account of metaphysical inquiry.

AT A GLANCE

Heidegger asks what makes metaphysical questioning possible at all. Rather than beginning with a catalogue of entities, he investigates the relation between beings as a whole and 'the nothing,' arguing that moods such as anxiety disclose a dimension of existence that ordinary scientific objectification does not capture.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Heidegger begin metaphysics by asking about the nothing rather than by defining what exists?

BEINGS AND BEING

Heidegger distinguishes particular beings from the question of Being—the fact and meaning of their appearing as beings at all. Metaphysics risks forgetting this distinction when it deals only with entities.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What question is missed when philosophy asks only what things exist?

SCIENCE

Science investigates determinate regions of beings through rigorous methods. Heidegger respects this work while arguing that science does not normally ask about the meaning of beings as a whole.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why can methodological success leave more fundamental questions untouched?

THE NOTHING

The lecture’s most famous claim is that the nothing is not simply another object. It becomes significant in the experience through which beings as a whole recede from their ordinary familiarity.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

ANXIETY

In anxiety, ordinary practical significance loosens and beings as a whole can appear strange or indifferent. This mood is philosophically important because it discloses rather than merely reports a psychological state.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How is Heideggerian anxiety different from fear of a specific thing?

NEGATION

Heidegger challenges the idea that the nothing is merely produced by logical negation. He reverses the relationship: our capacity to negate may depend on a more basic disclosure of nothingness.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does this reversal matter for his critique of logic as the final court of metaphysics?

TRANSCENDENCE

Human existence is not enclosed within isolated objects; it can relate to beings as a whole and ask what they mean. Heidegger calls this movement beyond particular beings transcendence.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What feature of human existence makes metaphysical questioning possible?

QUESTIONING

The ability to ask a radical question is itself philosophically significant. Metaphysics is not simply a body of doctrines but a mode of questioning in which the questioner is implicated.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Heidegger think genuine questioning changes the one who asks?

LOGIC

Heidegger’s treatment of the nothing provoked objections that his language violated logical sense. The lecture deliberately tests whether formal logic can exhaust the conditions of philosophical disclosure.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

LANGUAGE

Heidegger’s compressed and unconventional language is part of his attempt to loosen inherited conceptual habits.

THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION

The lecture culminates in the question: why are there beings at all rather than nothing? This question later becomes central to Heidegger’s account of metaphysics.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What makes this question different from asking for the cause of one particular thing?
