AT A GLANCE

Animal Farm retells the rise and corruption of a revolution through animals who overthrow human rule only to see a new elite emerge. The fable draws strongly on the Russian Revolution while also making broader claims about power and political language.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Orwell use a simple fable to address complex political history?

REVIEW — TEN CLAIMS TO REMEMBER

1. State the work’s central problem in one sentence.
2. Identify its strongest argument, image, character relationship, or formal achievement.
3. Distinguish what the work depicts from what it endorses.
4. Track the work’s major causal, conceptual, historical, or character relationships.
5. Identify one historical assumption or limitation.
6. Explain one major symbol, scene, technique, or distinction in the work’s own framework.
7. Connect the work to at least two earlier Great Books.
8. State the strongest objection or criticism.
9. Identify one insight that survives even if some elements have aged poorly.
10. Explain why the work still belongs in a Great Books curriculum.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Which claim, scene, or image from this work most changes the way you understand its subject?

DEEPER READING LABS

LAB 1 — ARGUMENT OR PLOT MAP
Map the central movement and identify the decisive choices, reversals, or interpretive turns.

LAB 2 — KEY DISTINCTIONS

LAB 3 — HISTORICAL TEST
