# v5.27 Focus Anchor Drag Fix

- Restores reliable drag-to-reposition behavior for the Focus Anchor in the normal reader.
- Tracks pointer movement at the window level so dragging continues even when the pointer crosses outside the overlay.
- Prevents the underlying reader click handler from taking over an anchor drag.
- Keeps the fullscreen Focus Anchor fixed in its dedicated top band, as designed.
- Persists the new anchor position immediately when dragging ends.
