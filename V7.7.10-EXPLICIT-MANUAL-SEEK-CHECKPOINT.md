# v7.7.10 — Explicit Manual Seek Checkpoint

## Fix

A deliberate click on a reader word now saves that exact word index directly. The session save no longer recalculates the checkpoint from the top visible word before Resume.

This specifically prevents Resume or an empty-area pause/resume click from returning the pointer to the top of the current viewport after a manual seek.

## Protected scope

No pagination, fullscreen, scrolling, virtualization, authentication, database, or general reader-location logic was changed.
