# v5.47 Progress & Awards Analytics

- Adds a separate Progress & Awards page under Library.
- Visual dashboard includes:
  - 30-day daily word line graph
  - 8-week reading-time bar graph
  - reading-mode pie chart
  - WPM trend line graph
- Adds statistical summaries for session size, duration, consistency, WPM improvement, books completed, and awards.
- Adds adjustable annual books-per-year goal and goal progress.
- Adds 12 trophy awards for first session, streaks, monthly consistency, words, comprehension, speed improvement, books completed, time, and annual goals.
- Adds private rule-based recommendations immediately.
- Adds optional OpenAI progress analysis through `/api/progress-recommendations`; it uses the existing server-side `OPENAI_API_KEY`.
- No raw book text is sent for progress recommendations—only aggregate metrics and recent daily/weekly summaries.
