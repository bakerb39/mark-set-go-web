# v7.0.13 Hybrid AI Reading Profile and Quick Book Guide

## Hybrid Reading Profile
- Adds an optional Enhance with AI action.
- Sends bounded text samples, metadata, transparent linguistic scores, and
  measurements to a dedicated server endpoint.
- AI independently evaluates textual, interpretive, contextual, and literary
  structure difficulty.
- Explicitly prevents literary prestige or depth from inflating syntax.
- Combines AI and local scores with dimension-specific weighting.
- Local linguistic analysis remains authoritative for textual difficulty.
- AI has greater influence on interpretation and contextual knowledge.
- Structured evidence identifies whether a finding came from the text sample,
  metadata, or established characteristics of the work.
- Results are cached locally and can be used without repeating the request.

## Quick Book Guide
- Adds an on-demand concise book guide.
- Includes overview, setting, characters, themes, structure, context, symbols,
  motifs, and reading tips.
- Offers spoiler-free, light-spoiler, and full-work modes.
- Generated only after the reader requests it.
- Cached locally by title and spoiler mode.
- Does not claim affiliation with a commercial study-guide publisher.

## Configuration
Both AI features use the existing OPENAI_API_KEY and
OPENAI_COMPREHENSION_MODEL server configuration.
