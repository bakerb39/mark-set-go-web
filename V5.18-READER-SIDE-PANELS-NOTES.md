# v5.18 Reader Side Panels

- Consolidates normal reader settings into a **Reader Controls** slide panel on the right.
- Reading and Display controls are moved into that panel without duplicating their IDs or event handlers.
- Learn section contains the Comprehension action.
- Media section contains Media Match, Music Score, Reading Mood, and preferred music behavior.
- Language & Words contains translation and word-result tools.
- Removes the standalone Media Match controls from the title area by moving the same live controls into the panel.
- Removes the standalone comprehension action from the playback row by moving the same live button into Learn.
- Left **Marks & Contents** slide panel now exposes tabs for Contents, Bookmarks, Saved Definitions, and Notes with item counts.
- Both side panels start closed to keep the reading canvas uncluttered; their labeled toggle buttons remain visible.
- Fullscreen retains the compact fullscreen Options menu.
