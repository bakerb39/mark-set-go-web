ECCLESIASTES
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Bible Guide
Biblical book: Ecclesiastes

Meaning, mortality, work, pleasure, wisdom, injustice, time, limits, enjoyment, and the fear of God.

HOW TO USE THIS BIBLE GUIDE
Use this guide alongside the biblical text. Read the assigned passages first whenever possible. The guide is designed to help with structure, literary context, theology, difficult passages, connections across Scripture, discussion, and comprehension—not to replace Scripture itself.

READING PRINCIPLES
Pay attention to genre. Poetry, wisdom sayings, lament, dialogue, and reflective argument communicate differently from narrative or legal prose. Distinguish description from endorsement, read passages in context, and allow the whole biblical canon to inform difficult texts.

PURPOSE AND PLACE IN SCRIPTURE

Ecclesiastes examines life 'under the sun' through the voice of Qoheleth, testing wisdom, pleasure, achievement, wealth, work, and reputation against the certainty of death and the limits of human knowledge.

STUDY QUESTION
Why does a biblical wisdom book repeatedly frustrate attempts to find permanent gain in ordinary achievement?

CHRIST AND THE REST OF SCRIPTURE

Ecclesiastes contributes to biblical realism about mortality and the limits of earthly achievement. Christian readers place its questions within the larger hope of resurrection and new creation.

STUDY QUESTION
