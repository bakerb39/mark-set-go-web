AT A GLANCE

Keynes challenges the view that competitive markets automatically restore full employment. An economy can settle into an equilibrium with persistent unemployment when total spending is insufficient.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is the possibility of underemployment equilibrium so disruptive to classical economic assumptions?

CLASSICAL THEORY

Keynes targets doctrines suggesting that flexible wages, interest rates, and prices naturally coordinate saving, investment, and employment.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

EFFECTIVE DEMAND

Firms hire and produce based on expected demand for output. If aggregate spending is weak, production and employment can remain below capacity.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why can falling wages fail to solve mass unemployment?

THE CONSUMPTION FUNCTION

Household consumption rises with income but typically by less than the full increase. This leaves a gap that investment must fill if total demand is to support full employment.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does saving create a coordination problem at the level of the whole economy?

THE MULTIPLIER

An increase in investment can generate repeated rounds of income and spending, producing a total effect larger than the initial expenditure.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does one person’s spending become another person’s income?

INVESTMENT

Investment depends on expected future returns, financing conditions, and confidence. Because the future is uncertain, investment can fluctuate sharply.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is investment more volatile than ordinary consumption?

UNCERTAINTY AND EXPECTATIONS

Keynes distinguishes calculable risk from deeper uncertainty about an unknowable future. Expectations therefore rely partly on conventions and confidence.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

ANIMAL SPIRITS

Entrepreneurial action often requires confidence beyond what can be justified by probability alone. Keynes calls this impulse animal spirits.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD

LIQUIDITY PREFERENCE

People may prefer holding money for transactions, precaution, or speculation. Interest is partly the reward required to give up liquidity.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How does preference for liquidity connect money markets to real economic activity?

THE RATE OF INTEREST

Keynes rejects the idea that interest simply balances saving and investment. Monetary conditions and liquidity preference play central roles.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does this reinterpretation change the role of monetary policy?

POLICY IMPLICATIONS

If private demand remains insufficient, public spending can support employment and output. Keynes does not argue that all government spending is always beneficial; the claim is conditional on macroeconomic circumstances.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
When is deficit spending most likely to have a strong stabilizing effect?

LEGACY

The General Theory reshaped macroeconomics and policy. Later schools modified, criticized, and integrated Keynesian ideas, but aggregate demand, expectations, and stabilization remain central concepts.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Which Keynesian ideas survived even in economic traditions that reject parts of his original framework?
