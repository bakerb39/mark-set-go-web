SCIENCE AND HYPOTHESIS
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Henri Poincaré

A guide to geometry, convention, physical law, hypothesis, measurement, mathematics, and Poincaré’s philosophy of science.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

AT A GLANCE

Poincaré asks how mathematics and physics achieve certainty, what role conventions play in geometry, and how scientific hypotheses organize experience. He rejects both naive empiricism and the idea that science is arbitrary.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Poincaré think some scientific principles are neither simple facts nor arbitrary inventions?

REVIEW — TEN CLAIMS TO REMEMBER

1. State the work’s central problem in one sentence.
2. Identify its strongest argument, insight, experiment, or explanatory move.
3. Distinguish description from endorsement.
4. Track the work’s major conceptual or causal relationships.
5. Identify one historical assumption or limitation.
6. Explain one major term, model, experiment, image, or distinction.
7. Connect the work to at least two earlier Great Books.
8. State the strongest objection or criticism.
9. Identify one insight that survives even if some details are outdated.
10. Explain why the work still belongs in a Great Books curriculum.
