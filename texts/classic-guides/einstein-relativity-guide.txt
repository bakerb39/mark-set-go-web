RELATIVITY: THE SPECIAL AND THE GENERAL THEORY
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Albert Einstein

A guide to frames of reference, simultaneity, time dilation, length contraction, spacetime, gravity, and Einstein’s conceptual revolution.

AT A GLANCE

Einstein’s popular exposition explains why measurements of space and time depend on the state of motion of observers, while the laws of physics retain invariant structure. General relativity then reconceives gravitation through the geometry of spacetime.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does relativity change the meaning of measurement without implying that 'everything is relative'?

REFERENCE FRAMES

Motion is described relative to a chosen frame. Uniform motion cannot be identified by a privileged absolute state of rest.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is relative motion different from saying there are no objective physical relationships?

THE PRINCIPLE OF RELATIVITY

The laws of physics take the same form in all inertial frames. No inertial observer can perform an internal experiment that reveals a unique absolute velocity.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What does it mean for different observers to disagree about measurements while obeying the same laws?

THE SPEED OF LIGHT

The speed of light in vacuum is invariant for inertial observers. Combining this with the relativity principle forces revisions to classical ideas of space and time.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why can’t classical velocity addition remain unchanged if light speed is invariant?

SIMULTANEITY

Events simultaneous in one inertial frame need not be simultaneous in another moving frame. Einstein operationalizes simultaneity through synchronized clocks and light signals.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does defining simultaneity require a physical procedure rather than intuition alone?

TIME DILATION

Moving clocks are measured to run slower relative to a given inertial frame. This is not a mechanical defect in clocks but a consequence of spacetime structure.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why is time dilation reciprocal for inertial observers without producing a contradiction?

LENGTH CONTRACTION

Lengths parallel to relative motion are measured differently by observers in different inertial frames. Spatial measurement therefore depends on simultaneity conventions.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why are length contraction and relativity of simultaneity inseparable?

SPACETIME

Minkowski’s formulation unifies space and time into a four-dimensional structure. Different observers divide spacetime into space and time differently while agreeing on invariant intervals.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What becomes invariant when separate measurements of space and time vary?

EQUIVALENCE PRINCIPLE

Einstein notices a deep connection between acceleration and gravity. Locally, the effects of a uniform gravitational field can resemble those of an accelerating frame.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why was the equivalence principle the conceptual bridge from special to general relativity?

GRAVITY AS GEOMETRY

General relativity replaces Newtonian gravitational force with curved spacetime geometry. Matter and energy shape geometry, while geometry shapes motion.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
How does a geometric account differ conceptually from a force acting instantaneously across space?

TESTS AND PREDICTIONS

The theory predicts effects such as gravitational redshift, light bending, and the anomalous precession of Mercury. Later observations greatly expanded the empirical confirmation of general relativity.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why are novel quantitative predictions especially important for revolutionary theories?

LEGACY

Relativity transformed physics, cosmology, astronomy, navigation, and philosophy of space and time. GPS technology, black-hole physics, gravitational-wave astronomy, and modern cosmology all depend on relativistic effects.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
What is the difference between relativity as a technical physical theory and the popular slogan that everything depends on perspective?
