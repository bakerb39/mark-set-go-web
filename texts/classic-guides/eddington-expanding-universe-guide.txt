THE EXPANDING UNIVERSE
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by Arthur Eddington

A guide to relativity, cosmology, redshift, expanding space, observational evidence, and the early twentieth-century transformation of our picture of the universe.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

HOW TO USE IT
Read one section at a time. Use Discuss with reading companion after each section for interpretation and Quiz me for a short comprehension check. Save the full-guide quiz for the end.

AT A GLANCE

Eddington explains the emerging idea that the universe is not static but expanding. Writing during the early development of relativistic cosmology, he connects Einstein’s equations, astronomical observations, and philosophical questions about cosmic structure.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why was an expanding universe conceptually harder to accept than a static one?
