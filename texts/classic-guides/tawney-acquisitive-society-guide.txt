THE ACQUISITIVE SOCIETY
An Independent Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide
Original work by R. H. Tawney

A guide to property, function, industry, inequality, social purpose, professionalism, and Tawney’s critique of acquisitive capitalism.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independently written educational guide is designed for serious first reading, rereading, and seminar study. It follows the Mark, Set, Go! Classic Guide pattern with focused sections, discussion prompts, section quizzes, Great Ideas, review, and whole-guide quiz hooks.

AT A GLANCE

Tawney argues that modern industrial society has confused property rights with social purpose. Economic institutions should be judged by the functions they perform for the community rather than by an unlimited right of acquisition.

QUESTION TO CARRY FORWARD
Why does Tawney think property needs social justification rather than merely legal protection?
