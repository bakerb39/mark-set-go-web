# v5.2 Preferred Music

- Added persistent Preferred Music list stored in localStorage.
- Music Library cards can be added to Preferred Music.
- Pasted YouTube videos/playlists can be named and added to Preferred Music.
- Music Library includes Play and Remove controls for saved items.
- Reader header includes a Preferred Music dropdown for quick switching.
- Fullscreen Options includes the same Preferred Music dropdown.
- Both reader dropdowns stay synchronized during the current reading session.
- Includes a Manage preferred music option that opens the Music page.
