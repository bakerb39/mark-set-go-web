# v6.1.8 Viewport-Independent WPM

## Principle
Selected WPM is now based on elapsed time and words advanced, not pixel
distance.

## Updated modes

### Auto Scroll
- Uses an elapsed-time word clock.
- Calculates the exact word index due at the selected WPM.
- Scrolls that word to a stable reading line.
- Viewer width, height, wrapping, and side-panel changes do not alter speed.

### Digital Sign
- Uses the same elapsed-time word clock as the authoritative position.
- Pixel movement is only a visualization of the current due word.
- Resizing repositions the active word but does not reset or alter WPM.

### Other guided modes
- Highlight, Bold Focus, Smooth Glide, Pointing Guide, Marquee, and Flash
  already schedule each step by actual words in the group.
- Meaningful chunks and line-limited pointing steps use their real word counts
  when calculating the next deadline.
- Deadlines are cumulative, preventing rendering or scroll time from gradually
  slowing the reader.

## Formula
At any time:

`target word = starting word + floor(elapsed minutes × selected WPM)`
