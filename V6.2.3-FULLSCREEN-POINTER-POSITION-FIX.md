# v6.2.3 Fullscreen Pointer Position Fix

## Problem
Entering fullscreen while Pointing Guide was running caused the pointer to
restart at the beginning of the document.

## Cause
Fullscreen changes the reader dimensions and word wrapping. The transition did
not explicitly preserve and restore the timed reader's canonical word index.

## Fix
- Captures the exact engine word index before entering or leaving fullscreen.
- Pauses playback during the layout transition.
- Restores the reader and pointer to the captured word after fullscreen settles.
- Resumes automatically when reading was active before the transition.
- Handles native fullscreen, fallback fullscreen, button exit, and Escape exit.
- Uses the same position restoration logic for Book Pages and standard scrolling.
