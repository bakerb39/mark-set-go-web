# v7.7.12 — Cursor and Viewport Separation

## Purpose

Separate the Pointing Guide playback cursor from the reader viewport anchor so saving or restoring the visible page cannot overwrite the hand cursor.

## Protected behavior

- `state.index` is now reserved for the timed-reader playback cursor.
- `state.viewportAnchorIndex` records the visible document anchor independently.
- `captureReaderLocation()` no longer assigns a viewport-derived value to `state.index`.
- Reader session snapshots store both `playbackIndex` and `viewportAnchorIndex`.
- Reader continuity captures and restores the viewport without replacing the active cursor.
- Clicking a word updates both positions intentionally and immediately persists the selected cursor.

## Scope

No Clerk, PostgreSQL, beta-gate, fullscreen, Book Pages geometry, or general rendering behavior was intentionally changed.
