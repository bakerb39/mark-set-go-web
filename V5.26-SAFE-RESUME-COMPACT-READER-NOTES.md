# v5.26 Safe Resume + Compact Reader Header

- Restores Marks & Contents and Reader Controls side-by-side on the left.
- Moves Full screen onto the same compact row on the right.
- Removes the separate fullscreen toolbar row and the "Open either side panel..." hint.
- Pulls the reading frame directly under the controls, eliminating the dead vertical band.

## Reader persistence fixes
- Background/session persistence now occurs only while the actual reader is mounted.
- Bible chapters and Bible books are saved immediately when opened in the Reader.
- Visiting Bible Study, Syntopicon, Library, etc. can no longer overwrite Resume Last Reading with a stale hidden large book.
- After a browser refresh, the top Reader action no longer silently reconstructs a potentially huge saved document. It returns to Home, where Resume Last Reading remains an explicit action.
