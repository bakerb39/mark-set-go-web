# v5.41 Bible Structure + Pointing Guide Book Pages

## Bible reader formatting
- Bible chapters now create an authoritative document structure instead of relying on generic heading detection.
- Verse lines can no longer become TOC entries merely because they begin with a number.
- Verse numbers render as small, subdued superscript-style numbers rather than bold section headings.
- Source paragraph/line breaks are preserved as reader paragraph spacing.
- Explicit Bible API headings become section headings.
- Chapter headings and API section headings are the only structural Bible items.

## Bible TOC
- Single chapter: authoritative Chapter entry plus explicit source section headings.
- Entire book: authoritative chapter + section TOC assembled with correct word offsets.
- Generic bold/numeric text is not used to invent Bible TOC entries.

## Pointing Guide + Book Pages
- Pointing Guide now moves to the spread containing its current word before calculating hand/line geometry.
- When the next pointing step crosses a spread boundary, Book Pages advances automatically.
- Other reading modes are unchanged.
