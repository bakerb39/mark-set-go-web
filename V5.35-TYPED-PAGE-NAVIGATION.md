# v5.35 Typed Page Navigation

- Adds a numeric page field to the bottom Book Pages controller.
- Readers can type a page number and press Enter, or change/blur the field, to jump directly there.
- Page numbers are clamped to the current valid range.
- Facing-page math remains synchronized: pages 1–2 = spread 1, 3–4 = spread 2, etc.
- After a typed jump, the logical reader word position is synchronized to the destination spread.
- The current page field and total page count update automatically after resize/reflow.
- The compact spread label remains visible on larger screens and hides on narrow screens.
