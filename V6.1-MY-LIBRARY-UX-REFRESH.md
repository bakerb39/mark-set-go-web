# v6.1 My Library UX Refresh

- Leaves the Home page unchanged.
- Rebuilds My Library around one primary task: continue reading.
- Adds a large current-book card with progress and Resume Reading.
- Consolidates annual goal, streak, today's reading, and comprehension into one card.
- Replaces the crowded statistics row and eight equal-weight cards.
- Adds larger, more readable Continue Reading cards.
- Moves administrative actions into a compact Quick Actions section.
- Organizes reading list, bookmarks, notes, vocabulary, and imports under My Collections.
- Moves detailed statistics into a collapsed Reading Insights section.
- Uses progressive disclosure, fewer borders, more white space, and meaningful color accents.
